Key issues identified by participants

Uranium supply
Suppliers stressed the need for predictable demand and long-term contractual commitments to support new investment in mining operations.

Conversion
Industry operators identified uncertainty over future volumes as one of the main obstacles to investment in new capacity and alternative conversion processes.

Enrichment
Enrichment service providers highlighted the long lead times required to license, finance and build capacity for next-generation fuels.

Fuel fabrication
Participants emphasised the need to subject new materials, compositions and manufacturing processes to full qualification programmes before their use in commercial reactors.

Research and validation
Representatives of research institutes stressed that technologies based on new physical principles or on phenomena not yet sufficiently characterised require prior independent experimental validation.
